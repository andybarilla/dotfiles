## Codebase Patterns
- Use `ansible_distribution` fact to detect distro (Ubuntu, Fedora, etc.)
- Detect distrobox via `DISTROBOX_ENTER_PATH` env var, not just `/run/.containerenv`
- Distrobox creates both `/run/.toolboxenv` and `/run/.containerenv` - use env var to distinguish
- Remove conflicting apt sources files before running apt operations (e.g., vscode.sources vs vscode.list)
- Skip flatpaks and host-level tasks when running from distrobox
- Ubuntu package names often differ from Fedora (e.g., `golang-go` vs `go`, `network-manager-openvpn` vs `NetworkManager-openvpn`)
